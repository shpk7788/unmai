Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 eyes major milestones across southern markets on day 11.

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 is projected to cross ₹90 crore in the Telugu states and ₹80 crore in Karnataka by its 11th day on Sunday, October 25, 2026. Box office tracker Jerin Georgekutty also reported that the sequel would cross ₹70 crore in Kerala, with combined southern collections outside Tamil Nadu exceeding ₹240 crore. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial is produced by Sun Pictures and features an ensemble cast including Mohanlal, Shiva Rajkumar, and S. J. Suryah. These figures are based on trade tracker updates and are not official statements from the production house.

Jailer 2 beats Leo to become second biggest Tamil grosser

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 has crossed the Rs 600 crore mark globally in eight days, becoming the fourth Tamil film to achieve this milestone.
