Jailer 2 nears Rs 600 crore mark after massive six-day run

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has grossed Rs 541 crore worldwide within six days of its release on October 15, 2026. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial now needs roughly Rs 64 crore to surpass the Rs 604.74 crore lifetime gross earned by the 2023 original film. Benefiting from the Dussehra holiday window, strong international markets with Rs 204 crore in overseas gross, and cameos by Shiva Rajkumar, Hrithik Roshan, and Mohanlal, the sequel recorded a higher Tuesday collection of Rs 45.55 crore nett in India compared to Monday.

Body double Joby Kodakara clarifies his role in Jailer 2 climax scene

Mimicry artist Joby Kodakara has confirmed that his hands and legs were used as a body double for Mohanlal in the climax of the Rajinikanth-starrer Jailer 2. The controversy erupted after Joby shared photos of himself dressed in Mohanlal's look on the film's release day. Addressing the claims, Joby clarified that "his face was not shown anywhere" in the movie and only specific body parts were used in a few shots.

Canada theatre cancels Jailer 2 screenings over security key dispute

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 will stop screening at Canada's York Cinemas starting October 21, 2026, due to a dispute over digital security keys. The theatre stated it had completed its payments, but North American distributors Shloka Entertainments and Prathyangira Cinemas failed to provide the required KDMs following authorization from production house Sun Pictures. York Cinemas confirmed that the screening halt was entirely beyond its control.
