Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 hits 544 crore rupees worldwide in six days

Superstar Rajinikanth's latest release Jailer 2 has grossed approximately 544 crore rupees worldwide within its first six days. In the Telugu states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, the movie collected 42.34 crore rupees, crossing its 42 crore break-even target to achieve hit status locally. Regionally, the film earned 132.10 crore rupees in Tamil Nadu, 55.05 crore rupees in Karnataka, 55.60 crore rupees in Kerala, 32.10 crore rupees from Hindi and ROI, and an estimated 202.60 crore rupees overseas. Against an overall worldwide business of 302 crore rupees and a break-even target of 304 crore rupees, the action entertainer needs another 43.90 crore rupees to reach full recovery.

Jailer 2 sets high box office bar as Ramayana advance bookings begin

Hrithik Roshan makes Tamil debut with a seven-minute cameo in Jailer 2

Bollywood superstar Hrithik Roshan makes his Tamil cinema debut playing a tactical arms operator named Roddy in Nelson Dilipkumar's Jailer 2. His high-impact cameo clocks in at approximately 7 minutes and intersects with the Kalpak syndicate. The film marks an on-screen reunion for Rajinikanth and Hrithik after exactly 40 years, following their 1986 Hindi classic Bhagwaan Dada. Shah Rukh Khan was initially discussed for the sequence before scheduling conflicts led the makers to cast Hrithik Roshan.
