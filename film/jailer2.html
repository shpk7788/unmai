Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses ₹500 crore worldwide in six days

Rajinikanth's action drama Jailer 2 crossed ₹542 crore worldwide within six days of its October 15 release. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and produced by Sun Pictures, the sequel has become the third-highest-grossing Indian film of 2026. The film's total net collection in India reached ₹289.05 crore, while overseas gross touched ₹204 crore. With this milestone, Rajinikanth becomes the only actor in the Tamil film industry to deliver four films crossing the ₹500 crore mark.

Canada theatre cancels Jailer 2 screenings over security key dispute

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 will stop screening at Canada's York Cinemas starting October 21, 2026, due to a dispute over digital security keys.
