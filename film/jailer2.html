Jailer 2 aims to cross 400 crore worldwide in four days

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 is on course to cross 400 crore worldwide during its extended four-day opening weekend. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial grossed approximately 321 crore globally in its first three days, with strong Sunday bookings and overseas demand offering a chance to reach 425 crore. Overseas markets account for about 48.50% of the worldwide total, reducing reliance on India alone. Within India, the film grossed 70.25 crore on Thursday, 43.40 crore on Friday, and 55.35 crore on Saturday. Sunday's pre-sales closed at 31.38 crore gross across 16,263 shows.
