Jailer 2 makes history with midnight Tamil shows in North India

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has made history as the first South Indian film to receive post-midnight theatrical screenings in original Tamil across North India. Driven by high demand, independent multiplexes and single screens in Mumbai and Pune added late-night slots while reducing shows for competing films like Nayyi Navelli and Prahaar. The Hindi version earned an estimated Rs. 16.50 crore net during its four-day extended opening weekend, while worldwide collections crossed Rs. 300 crore in three days. This expansion happened despite major national chains skipping the film due to a streaming window dispute.
