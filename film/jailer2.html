Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses ₹765 crore worldwide on day 15

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 earned ₹5.90 crore net in India on its fifteenth day, taking its domestic net collection to ₹429.55 crore and total worldwide gross past ₹765.15 crore. The Tamil version contributed ₹3.20 crore, followed by ₹1.40 crore from the Telugu version and ₹1.10 crore from the Hindi version. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial faced distribution issues in northern India due to an OTT window disagreement between PVR-Inox and the makers. Sun Pictures produced the action entertainer featuring an ensemble cast alongside Rajinikanth.

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Projections suggest a 125 crore worldwide start for the Nelson Dilipkumar-directed action entertainer. The film recorded 41.50 crores gross in India bookings, selling 19.60 lakh tickets across 17,615 shows, with worldwide pre-sales finishing around 75 crores. While early audience reactions are positive, opinions on the second half are divided.

Why Santhanam was cut from Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2

Comedian Santhanam was completely cut from Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 because makers found his sequence unnecessary. Dubbing engineer Godwin revealed that Santhanam played an event manager who arranged a song during a birthday party scene. Despite deleting several portions featuring big stars, the Nelson directorial grossed 777 crore worldwide to become the highest-grossing Tamil film of all time. Daily collections have now dropped below ten crore.
