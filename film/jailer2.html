Jailer child star Rithvik Jothi Raj starts his own company at 13

Thirteen-year-old actor Rithvik Jothi Raj, known for playing Rajinikanth's grandson in Jailer and Jailer 2, has launched his own company with a group of friends. In an interview, he explained that the venture began out of boredom during video calls when they decided to build a website using basic coding skills. The young actor, who rose to fame through his YouTube channel RithuRocks, confirmed that the startup currently has low traffic but continues to operate. Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 nears Rs 500 crore in five days

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 is dominating the box office, racing towards the Rs 500-crore mark globally within just five days of release. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the film recorded the highest-ever Monday collection in Tamil cinema history by earning Rs 42.35 crore net on day five due to festive holidays. This massive performance propelled the film's worldwide gross to Rs 477 crore, making it the third-highest-grossing Indian film of 2026 so far. The sequel features returning stars like Ramya Krishnan alongside new additions such as S. J. Suryah and high-profile cameos.

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 makes strong U.K. and Ireland box office debut

Rajinikanth's blockbuster Jailer 2 secured third place at the U.K. and Ireland box office, collecting $1.6 million during its debut week. Meanwhile, Sony's psychological thriller Verity moved down to fourth spot with $1.5 million.
