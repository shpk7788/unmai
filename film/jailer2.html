Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Projections suggest a 125 crore worldwide start for the Nelson Dilipkumar-directed action entertainer. The film recorded 41.50 crores gross in India bookings, selling 19.60 lakh tickets across 17,615 shows, with worldwide pre-sales finishing around 75 crores. While early audience reactions are positive, opinions on the second half are divided.

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 sees box office dip on second Monday

Rajinikanth's blockbuster sequel Jailer 2 witnessed a significant drop of over 55 percent in its Hindi version on its second Monday, earning Rs 1.4 crore. Jailer 2 crosses Rs 408 crore net at the domestic box office

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 surpassed Rs 408.15 crore in domestic net collections within 13 days of its October 15 release. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial achieved a worldwide gross total of Rs 733.35 crore, led by strong earnings from the Tamil version. The Hindi release encountered hurdles after PVR-Inox theatres in northern India declined screenings over a streaming window dispute. Latha Srinivasan described the film in her NDTV review as a "commercial entertainer featuring humour, action and music."

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 breaks Baahubali 2's nine-year Kerala box office record

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has collected ₹72.85 crore gross in Kerala by Day 13, overtaking Baahubali 2's lifetime collection of ₹72.25 crore. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial is now the highest-grossing non-Malayalam film in Kerala box office history, ending a benchmark set in 2017. Major pan-India blockbusters like KGF 2 and Leo previously failed to surpass this record. The film achieved massive numbers helped by Mohanlal's association and ₹1.61 crore in advance bookings within the first hour.
