Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Projections suggest a 125 crore worldwide start for the Nelson Dilipkumar-directed action entertainer. Hrithik Roshan makes his Tamil debut in Rajinikanth's Jailer 2

Bollywood star Hrithik Roshan makes his Tamil cinema debut with a high-impact cameo in Nelson Dilipkumar's Jailer 2, playing an international arms operator named Roddy. His action sequence clocks in at approximately 7 minutes of screen time, interacting with a syndicate headed by Mithun Chakraborty and Vidya Balan. This appearance marks a 40-year reunion for Hrithik and Rajinikanth, who last shared the screen as child artist and star in the 1986 Hindi classic Bhagwaan Dada. Shah Rukh Khan was reportedly considered initially, but scheduling conflicts led the makers to cast Hrithik instead.

Jailer 2 beats Leo to become the fastest Tamil film to hit 200 crore

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has become the fastest Tamil film to cross ₹200 crore in Tamil-language net collections, achieving the milestone in just six days to surpass Vijay's Leo, which took seven days. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the sequel grossed a massive ₹541 crore worldwide in six days, with ₹337 crore coming from India and ₹204 crore from overseas. A Vijayadashami holiday boost on Tuesday added ₹31.20 crore in Tamil net alone, taking its domestic Tamil version total to ₹206.30 crore.
