Jailer 2 child actor Rithvik Jothi Raj starts business venture due to boredom

Rajinikanth blockbuster Jailer 2 locks November 12 OTT premiere date on Prime Video

Rajinikanth's blockbuster action-crime sequel Jailer 2 is heading for an OTT premiere on Prime Video on November 12. The film originally opened in theatres on October 15.

Jailer 2 hits OTT after collecting over Rs 820 crore worldwide

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 made its OTT debut on Amazon Prime Video four weeks after its theatrical release on October 15. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial collected over Rs 820 crore worldwide. The four-week digital release window reportedly led national multiplex chains PVR INOX and Cinepolis to decline screening the film due to their eight-week requirement. Distributor Tiruppur Subramaniam had requested Sun Pictures to postpone the digital premiere, but Amazon Prime Video confirmed the November 12 release date.
