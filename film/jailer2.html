Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 dominates box office with massive opening weekend numbers

Hrithik Roshan makes his Tamil debut in Rajinikanth's Jailer 2

Bollywood star Hrithik Roshan makes his Tamil cinema debut with a high-impact cameo in Nelson Dilipkumar's Jailer 2, playing an international arms operator named Roddy. His action sequence clocks in at approximately 7 minutes of screen time, interacting with a syndicate headed by Mithun Chakraborty and Vidya Balan. This appearance marks a 40-year reunion for Hrithik and Rajinikanth, who last shared the screen as child artist and star in the 1986 Hindi classic Bhagwaan Dada. Shah Rukh Khan was reportedly considered initially, but scheduling conflicts led the makers to cast Hrithik instead.
