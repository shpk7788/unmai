Sanjeev Kumar Bijli praises Rajinikanth following Jailer 2 opening weekend success

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 achieved strong box office numbers, recording 1.3 million admissions during its opening weekend at PVR INOX. PVR INOX Executive Director Sanjeev Kumar Bijli stated that the numbers proved the star's box office pull. Written and directed by Nelson, the film features Rajinikanth returning as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian to tackle an idol smuggling syndicate. The sequel stars a large ensemble cast including Mohanlal, Shivarajkumar, Vidya Balan, and SJ Suryah.

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Jailer 2 makes history with midnight Tamil shows in North India

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has made history as the first South Indian film to receive post-midnight theatrical screenings in original Tamil across North India. Driven by high demand, independent multiplexes and single screens in Mumbai and Pune added late-night slots while reducing shows for competing films like Nayyi Navelli and Prahaar. The Hindi version earned an estimated Rs. 16.50 crore net during its four-day extended opening weekend, while worldwide collections crossed Rs. 300 crore in three days. This expansion happened despite major national chains skipping the film due to a streaming window dispute.
