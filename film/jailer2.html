Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 opens with 129 crore rupees worldwide

Rajinikanth's latest release Jailer 2 has pulled in an estimated 129 crore rupees in worldwide collections on its first day. The action entertainer grossed 24.85 crore rupees in Tamil Nadu, 15.65 crore rupees across the Telugu states, 14.60 crore rupees in Karnataka, and 56.50 crore rupees overseas. The film's overall worldwide theatrical business is valued at 302 crore rupees, with a break-even target of 304 crore rupees.

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 breaks Tamil opening records in Karnataka and Kerala

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 opened ahead of the 2023 original at the Indian and worldwide box office on October 16, 2026. The Nelson Dilipkumar-directed sequel set new all-time opening-day records for a Tamil film in Karnataka and Kerala, with Kerala recording the biggest absolute improvement. While Tamil Nadu remains the largest market, its growth was modest compared to other regions. International markets expanded faster than domestic business, contributing more than half of the sequel's additional worldwide opening collection.

Rajinikanth film Jailer 2 sets record opening day box office in Kerala

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 earned a record first-day gross of about ₹12.65 crore in Kerala, surpassing Vijay's Leo, which previously held the record with ₹12 crore. Exhibitors arranged extended shows for 18 continuous hours to manage heavy demand and strong advance bookings. FEUOK executive committee member Suresh Shenoy stated that this is a record for a Tamil film in the state. Theatres expect strong collections to continue through the Navaratri holidays.

Jailer 2 proves Rajinikanth needs zero punches to blow minds

Nelson Dilipkumar directs Jailer 2, featuring Rajinikanth as Muthu hunting an idol-smuggling network while working as a driver for a local councillor named Moorthy. At 75, the superstar throws almost no punches, relying on stillness, spectacles, snipers, and Anirudh's music to bring theatres to their feet. The film also features a clever doubter named Kohli and a second-half callback to Baasha. The story follows Muthu's quiet operations and eventual reveals in tea shops and pre-interval confrontations.

Jailer 2 Day 6 advance bookings cross Rs 3.4 crore in India

Jailer 2 has grossed ₹3.48 crore in advance bookings for its sixth day from 2.26 lakh tickets across 3,505 shows. Tamil Nadu leads state contributions with ₹2.07 crore, while Chennai stands as the top city with ₹92.74 lakh. Tamil language shows account for 67 percent of the gross, followed by Telugu at 25 percent and Hindi at 7 percent. Across all seven days on sale, the film has accumulated ₹80.23 crore in advance bookings.
