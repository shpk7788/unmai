Jailer 2 beats Jailer to become the highest-grossing Tamil film ever

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has emerged as the highest-grossing Tamil film of all time by collecting approximately Rs 744 crore worldwide within 14 days of its October 15 release. The sequel outpaces the 2023 original film Jailer, which earned around Rs 604.74 crore globally. While both films directed by Nelson Dilipkumar share similar narrative formulas and minor second-half pacing issues, Jailer 2 wins the fan debate through amplified execution, higher emotional stakes, and a thriller-like setup.
