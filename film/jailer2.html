Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses 700 crore worldwide

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has made box-office history by surpassing ₹730 crore worldwide within 11 days of release. In its home state of Tamil Nadu, the film has reportedly grossed over ₹175 crore and is now eyeing the ₹200 crore club. If it reaches this target, it will join previous major hits like Leo and Ponniyin Selvan: Part 1.
