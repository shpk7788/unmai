Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Jailer 2 hits ₹190 crore overseas in four days and targets records

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 grossed ₹190 crore overseas in just four days, reaching nearly 90 percent of its reported $21.5 to 22 million recovery target. The Nelson Dilipkumar-directed sequel surpassed Coolie's entire international run and is chasing the lifetime records of Jailer and Leo. In North America, the film crossed $5 million after four days, closing in on its $6.5 million break-even target. The Tamil version contributed $3.59 million and the Telugu version brought in $640,000 toward the total.

Jailer 2 crosses ₹234 crore in four days, targeting Leo's Kerala record

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 earned a massive ₹234.25 crore gross across India in its first four days. Kerala, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh-Telangana each crossed ₹40 crore, contributing heavily to the total. The Tamil version reached ₹143.70 crore net, positioning it to potentially enter the ₹150 crore net club by Day 5.
