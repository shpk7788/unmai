Jailer 2 matches Vikram box office record in Kerala

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has retained the No. 1 spot at the Kerala weekend box office for three consecutive weekends. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, it is only the second post-COVID film after Kamal Haasan's Vikram to maintain the top position for three successive weekends in the state. Released on October 15, 2026, the film crossed a gross collection of ₹76.25 crore in Kerala by Day 17. The sequel has already surpassed the lifetime collections of Baahubali 2 and Leo in the territory.

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses Rs 800 crore worldwide

Rajinikanth's action comedy Jailer 2 has grossed Rs 8.08 billion globally in 18 days, surpassing his 2018 film 2.0 as the highest-grossing Tamil film. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and produced by Sun Pictures, the sequel opened on October 15 and earned Rs 5.33 billion domestically. It also became the first Tamil film to collect Rs 750 million in each of the four south Indian markets. Distributor Tiruppur Subramaniam stated, 'I believe the movie will achieve Rs 1,000 crore.'

Jailer 2 crosses Rs 800 crore to become highest-grossing Tamil film

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has crossed Rs 807.25 crore worldwide within 18 days of release, becoming the first Tamil film to enter the Rs 800-crore club. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial surpassed the lifetime domestic record of Rajinikanth's own 2.0 to become Kollywood's highest-grossing film in India. Domestic net earnings stand at Rs 456.30 crore, while overseas markets contributed Rs 273.50 crore. The Tamil version led domestic collections with Rs 316.50 crore, followed by Telugu, Hindi, and Kannada versions.
