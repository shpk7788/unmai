Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses 500 crore rupees in six days

Rajinikanth's action drama Jailer 2 has crossed the ₹500 crore mark worldwide within six days of its October 15 release. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the sequel has reached a worldwide gross collection of ₹542 crore, making it the third-highest-grossing Indian film of 2026. The film recorded an India net collection of ₹289.05 crore alongside ₹204 crore from overseas markets. With this milestone, Rajinikanth has become the only actor in the Tamil film industry to deliver four films crossing ₹500 crore globally.
