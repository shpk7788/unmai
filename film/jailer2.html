Latest Jailer 2 news

Jailer 2 crosses Rs 765 crore worldwide on its fifteenth day

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 reached a worldwide gross of Rs 765.15 crore in 15 days, outperforming his previous release Coolie. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the film added Rs 5.90 crore net in India on Thursday, October 29, bringing its domestic net total to Rs 429.55 crore. The Tamil version led the collections on Day 15 with Rs 3.20 crore, followed by Telugu, Hindi, and Kannada versions. The sequel has already surpassed the lifetime worldwide collection of 2.0 to become the highest-grossing Tamil film ever.

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Projections suggest a 125 crore worldwide start for the Nelson Dilipkumar-directed action entertainer. The film recorded 41.50 crores gross in India bookings, selling 19.60 lakh tickets across 17,615 shows, with worldwide pre-sales finishing around 75 crores. While early audience reactions are positive, opinions on the second half are divided.

Rajinikanth shoots comedy sequences for his upcoming film Dharman

Following the success of Jailer 2, Superstar Rajinikanth is currently busy filming comedy scenes for his next movie, Dharman. Directed by Ashwath Marimuthu, the entertainer features Rajinikanth playing a doctor. Kamal Haasan is producing the project under Raaj Kamal Films International alongside R. Mahendran. The film's ensemble cast includes Simran, Raashii Khanna, Yogi Babu, and Nayanthara, with Anirudh Ravichander composing the music and Niketh Bommi handling the cinematography.

Jatin Sarna thought the WhatsApp offer for Jailer 2 was fake

Actor Jatin Sarna initially suspected that a WhatsApp message offering him a villainous role in Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 was fake. After previously playing a small cameo in Darbar, he received a request for photographs from an assistant director and verified the sender's identity on IMDb before responding. Director Nelson Dilipkumar ultimately cast Sarna after appreciating his earlier work in Sacred Games. Sarna expressed disbelief at getting substantial scenes and dialogue with Rajinikanth this time around, calling the opportunity a dream come true.

Rajinikanth film Jailer 2 hits lowest daily collection on day 14

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 recorded its lowest single-day collection on its 14th day with ₹6.55 crore. Released on October 15, 2026, the action drama has grossed a total of ₹423 crore in India following a strong ₹59.70 crore opening day. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial earned ₹334.35 crore in its first week before experiencing a midweek slowdown in ticket sales. The film co-stars Ramya Krishnan and Vidya Balan with music by Anirudh Ravichander.
