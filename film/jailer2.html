Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses ₹7 crore in overseas advance bookings

Rajinikanth's upcoming action drama Jailer 2 has crossed ₹7.70 crore in overseas opening-day advance bookings ahead of its worldwide release on October 15. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the sequel is aiming to surpass Coolie as the biggest opening-day grosser for a Kollywood film. Trade tracker Jerin Georgekutty reported the milestone on October 7, noting that only limited shows have been opened internationally so far. The film features returning cast members alongside new additions like S. J. Suryah and Vidya Balan, with music by Anirudh Ravichander.
