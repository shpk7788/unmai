Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Jailer 2 actor Namo Narayana quit his IT career for cinema

Actor Namo Narayana, known for playing Moorthy in Rajinikanth's Jailer 2, left behind a lucrative software career in the US to pursue filmmaking. He initially worked as an executive producer on the 2009 film Nadodigal before stumbling into acting when director Samuthirakani created a small role for him. Over 15 years, he appeared in more than 80 films before achieving a major breakout in Nelson's Jailer 2.

Jailer 2 targets 600 crore worldwide target with massive 4000 theater release

Rajinikanth-starrer Jailer 2 has been made on an overall budget of 300 crores, requiring 600 crores worldwide to achieve hit status. The movie opens across 4000 theaters globally, including 2500 in India and 1500 overseas, alongside 8500 shows in India on its first day. It recorded a 75 crore worldwide advance booking collection, featuring 38 crores from Hindi and 37 crores from overseas markets, while opening with 43 percent occupancy across Indian shows. The action film also features special cameos by Hrithik Roshan, Mohanlal, and Jackie Shroff.

Jailer 2 crosses ₹600 crore worldwide as action director confirms Mohanlal's climax role

Nelson Dilipkumar's Jailer 2 has grossed ₹606 crore worldwide after eight days at the box office. The Rajinikanth-starrer previously crossed ₹500 crore globally in five days, with Sacnilk reporting a Day 6 India net collection of ₹45.55 crore. Following viewer questions about a body double in the film's climax, action director Chethan Ramshi D’Souza confirmed on social media that it was indeed Mohanlal in the car scene.

23 Oct 2026 · Reported by iDiva

Jailer 2 sets new record with massive first week collection

Sun Pictures announced that Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 crossed 549 crore worldwide in seven days, becoming the highest-grossing Tamil film in first-week collections. Box office tracker Sacnilk reports that the Nelson directorial has earned around 600 crore overall. The action film features Rajinikanth reprising his role as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian alongside an ensemble cast including SJ Suryah, Mohanlal, and a special appearance by Hrithik Roshan. Producer Kalanithi Maran praised the lead star, describing him as “The Box Office Emperor”.
