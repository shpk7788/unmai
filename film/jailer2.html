Mohanlal associates unhappy with trimmed role in Rajinikanth's Jailer 2

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 released on October 15, but discontent has grown over the trimmed screen time of Malayalam star Mohanlal, whose gangster character Mathew had less presence than expected. Antony Perumbavoor, a long-time associate of Mohanlal, publicly voiced his displeasure at an event in Kochi, stating, "That isn't (something) happy for us Malayalis. We share that unhappiness too." Mohanlal has remained silent and hasn't shared film updates on social media, while a body double's viral photos briefly sparked unconfirmed rumours of a replacement during the climax.

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 nears Rs 500 crore at Indian box office

Rajinikanth's action drama Jailer 2 is approaching the Rs 500 crore club at the Indian box office. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial grossed Rs 475 crore in 15 days, adding Rs 101 crore during its second week. Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses ₹765 crore worldwide on day 15

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 earned ₹5.90 crore net in India on its fifteenth day, taking its domestic net collection to ₹429.55 crore and total worldwide gross past ₹765.15 crore. The Tamil version contributed ₹3.20 crore, followed by ₹1.40 crore from the Telugu version and ₹1.10 crore from the Hindi version. The Nelson Dilipkumar directorial faced distribution issues in northern India due to an OTT window disagreement between PVR-Inox and the makers. Sun Pictures produced the action entertainer featuring an ensemble cast alongside Rajinikanth.

Why Balakrishna turned down a major role in Jailer 2

Balakrishna was initially approached to play a powerful Goa kingpin in Rajinikanth's Jailer 2, but the role was eventually dropped. According to industry reports, he declined the offer after insiders warned him that Nagarjuna was given a similar powerful role in Coolie that was not portrayed effectively. Rather than replacing Balakrishna with another actor, the makers removed the character entirely from the film. The planned role was intended to be introduced as a ruthless villain before being revealed as a hero who saves Shivarajkumar's family.
