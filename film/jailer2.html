Suraj and Mirnaa roast Jailer 2 song lyrics in front of Anirudh

Actors Suraj Venjaramoodu and Mirnaa humorously questioned the Malayalam lyrics of the track Ala Bolelo from Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 in front of composer Anirudh Ravichander and director Nelson Dilipkumar. Suraj pointedly asked, "Ala Bolelo, Pitha Bolelo what Malayalam is this even?" while Mirnaa added that they could not find the words in any dictionary.
