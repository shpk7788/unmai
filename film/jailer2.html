Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Projections suggest a 125 crore worldwide start for the Nelson Dilipkumar-directed action entertainer. The film recorded 41.50 crores gross in India bookings, selling 19.60 lakh tickets across 17,615 shows, with worldwide pre-sales finishing around 75 crores. While early audience reactions are positive, opinions on the second half are divided.

Hrithik Roshan returns to share the screen with Rajinikanth in Jailer 2 cameo

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 rakes in big opening despite minimal promotions

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 opened to mixed reviews but pulled in an estimated Rs 125 crore worldwide on its first day. In the Telugu market, the film managed an opening of around Rs 15 crore gross, which is considered a modest start given its pre-release business. The movie capitalized on the Dasara holidays and a lack of major Tamil competition. Trade sources note that the upcoming release of Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika's Ranabaali may impact its future run in Telugu states.
