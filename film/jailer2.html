Jailer 2 breaks Leo record to become top Tamil overseas grosser

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has overtaken Thalapathy Vijay's Leo to become the highest-grossing Tamil film internationally in just seven days. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the action entertainer accumulated an estimated 205 crore overseas gross, surpassing the 204 crore benchmark established by Leo in 2023. Strong markets included the Middle East with $7.5 million and North America with $5.90 million. Produced by Sun Pictures, the film features Rajinikanth alongside Mohanlal, Shiva Rajkumar, Ramya Krishnan, and Vidya Balan.

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 racks up massive numbers in first week

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has grossed ₹582.45 crore worldwide by the end of its first week at the box office. The film pulled in ₹377 crore from India and another ₹205 crore from international markets. In domestic language breakdowns, the Tamil version earned ₹236.15 crore, the Telugu version brought in ₹66.36 crore, Hindi collected ₹32.02 crore, and Kannada contributed ₹3.77 crore. The movie is set to become a new industry hit for Kollywood, though reaching the ₹1000 crore mark remains a distant dream.
