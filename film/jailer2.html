Jailer 2 Day 6 advance bookings cross Rs 3.4 crore in India

Jailer 2 has grossed ₹3.48 crore in advance bookings for its sixth day from 2.26 lakh tickets across 3,505 shows. Tamil Nadu leads state contributions with ₹2.07 crore, while Chennai stands as the top city with ₹92.74 lakh. Tamil language shows account for 67 percent of the gross, followed by Telugu at 25 percent and Hindi at 7 percent. Across all seven days on sale, the film has accumulated ₹80.23 crore in advance bookings.

Producer Suryadevara Naga Vamsi celebrates Jailer 2 success in Telugu states

Producer and distributor Suryadevara Naga Vamsi expressed happiness over the box office performance of Jailer 2 in the Telugu states. The sequel, starring Rajinikanth and directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, was released on October 15 by Sun Pictures and distributed in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana by Sithara Entertainments. Naga Vamsi noted that despite initial concerns over the high acquisition price, the film performed strongly and likely reached its break-even point by Vijayadashami. "Whether it is a film we produce or one we distribute, it gives us immense happiness when distributors earn their commission," Naga Vamsi stated at a press meet.

Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 crosses Rs 325 crores worldwide in three days

Rajinikanth's action thriller Jailer 2, directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, has collected Rs. 325 crores at the worldwide box office in just three days. The film opened with Rs. 130 crore on Thursday, followed by Rs. 85 crore on the second day and an estimated Rs. 110 crore on the third day. It is now racing towards a massive extended opening weekend of over Rs. 425 crores, aiming to set a new record for Tamil cinema.
