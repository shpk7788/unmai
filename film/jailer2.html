Jailer 2 Day 6 advance bookings cross Rs 3.4 crore in India

Jailer 2 has grossed ₹3.48 crore in advance bookings for its sixth day from 2.26 lakh tickets across 3,505 shows. Tamil Nadu leads state contributions with ₹2.07 crore, while Chennai stands as the top city with ₹92.74 lakh. Tamil language shows account for 67 percent of the gross, followed by Telugu at 25 percent and Hindi at 7 percent. Across all seven days on sale, the film has accumulated ₹80.23 crore in advance bookings.

Rajinikanth and Hrithik Roshan reunite in Jailer 2 after forty years

Rajinikanth and Hrithik Roshan share screen space again in Jailer 2, forty years after their first film. The two previously starred together in the 1986 Hindi movie Bhagwan Dada, directed by J Om Prakash. In that film, a young Hrithik played Rajinikanth's adopted son as a child artist. Reflecting on the experience, Hrithik recalled how the veteran star used to take the blame for his dialogue delivery mistakes to protect his confidence. Hrithik Roshan said, Whenever I used to make a mistake in the dialogue... Rajni sir used to say, 'Sorry sorry, I made a mistake.'
