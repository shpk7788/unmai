Suraj and Mirnaa roast Jailer 2 song lyrics in front of Anirudh

Actors Suraj Venjaramoodu and Mirnaa humorously questioned the Malayalam lyrics of the track Ala Bolelo from Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 in front of composer Anirudh Ravichander and director Nelson Dilipkumar. The song had previously faced criticism from some Malayali listeners over its linguistic authenticity and pronunciation. Suraj pointedly asked, "Ala Bolelo, Pitha Bolelo what Malayalam is this even?" while Mirnaa added that they could not find the words in any dictionary. Nelson explained he had asked for a song sounding like Malayalam without knowing if the lyrics met that requirement. Directed by Nelson and produced by Sun Pictures, the film releases in theatres on October 15.
