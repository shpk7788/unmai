Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Projections suggest a 125 crore worldwide start for the Nelson Dilipkumar-directed action entertainer. The film recorded 41.50 crores gross in India bookings, selling 19.60 lakh tickets across 17,615 shows, with worldwide pre-sales finishing around 75 crores. While early audience reactions are positive, opinions on the second half are divided.

Kamal Haasan congratulates Rajinikanth and the team on Jailer 2 success

Actor and MP Kamal Haasan took to social media to congratulate Rajinikanth and the team behind Jailer 2 on the film's success. Kamal posted on X, mentioning that he heard reports of the film's success and congratulated his friend Rajinikanth, director Nelson, music composer Anirudh, and producers Sun Pictures. The film is written and directed by Nelson, with music by Anirudh. In the sequel, Rajinikanth returns as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian to track down a larger syndicate behind the idol smuggling plot from the 2023 film Jailer.

Jailer 2 Day 6 advance bookings cross Rs 3.4 crore in India

Jailer 2 has grossed ₹3.48 crore in advance bookings for its sixth day from 2.26 lakh tickets across 3,505 shows. Tamil Nadu leads state contributions with ₹2.07 crore, while Chennai stands as the top city with ₹92.74 lakh. Tamil language shows account for 67 percent of the gross, followed by Telugu at 25 percent and Hindi at 7 percent. Across all seven days on sale, the film has accumulated ₹80.23 crore in advance bookings.

Rajinikanth commands massive remuneration and profit share for Jailer 2

Superstar Rajinikanth received a base remuneration of Rs 110 crore for Jailer 2, with profit-sharing potentially pushing his total earnings to nearly Rs 180 crore. The action sequel also features a high-profile cast charging substantial fees. Hrithik Roshan charged Rs 25 crore for his cameo appearance. Vidya Balan joined the franchise for Rs 6 crore, while Shiva Rajkumar commands a fee of Rs 8 crore to reprise his role.

Jailer 2 secures a massive 75 crore worldwide advance before release

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 is hitting the big screen with a massive 75 crores in worldwide advance bookings, consisting of 38 crores in Hindi and 37 crores overseas. The action-packed sequel features cameos by Hrithik Roshan, Mohanlal, and Jackie Shroff. Made on a budget of 300 crores with a distribution target of 600 crores to be termed a hit, the movie is opening across 4,000 theaters worldwide, including 2,500 in India with 8,500 day-one shows and a 43 percent occupancy rate.

Jailer 2 smashes Leo record for peak-hour ticket sales on BookMyShow

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has set a new record on BookMyShow for the highest single-hour ticket sales ever recorded for a Tamil film. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the action entertainer surpassed Vijay's 2023 film Leo, which previously held the top spot. The milestone was achieved during the film's opening day on October 15, 2026, driven by strong rushes in markets like Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Karnataka. The ranking also includes Rajinikanth's Coolie and the original Jailer.
