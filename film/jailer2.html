Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Projections suggest a 125 crore worldwide start for the Nelson Dilipkumar-directed action entertainer. The film recorded 41.50 crores gross in India bookings, selling 19.60 lakh tickets across 17,615 shows, with worldwide pre-sales finishing around 75 crores. While early audience reactions are positive, opinions on the second half are divided.

Jailer 2 Day 6 advance bookings cross Rs 3.4 crore in India

Jailer 2 has grossed ₹3.48 crore in advance bookings for its sixth day from 2.26 lakh tickets across 3,505 shows. Tamil Nadu leads state contributions with ₹2.07 crore, while Chennai stands as the top city with ₹92.74 lakh. Tamil language shows account for 67 percent of the gross, followed by Telugu at 25 percent and Hindi at 7 percent. Across all seven days on sale, the film has accumulated ₹80.23 crore in advance bookings.

Producer Suryadevara Naga Vamsi celebrates Jailer 2 success in Telugu states

Producer and distributor Suryadevara Naga Vamsi expressed happiness over the box office performance of Jailer 2 in the Telugu states. Subhash K Jha slams Rajinikanth starrer Rajini Jailer 2 as a mindless sequel

Subhash K Jha published a scathing review of Rajini Jailer 2, directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and starring Rajinikanth. The critic blasted the sequel for lacking narrative coherence and relying entirely on random action sequences and excessive hero-worship of Rajinikanth's character, Tiger Muthuvel Pandian. The star-studded cast features Vidya Balan, Mithun Chakraborty, Suraj Venjaramoodu, S J Suryah, and Hrithik Roshan, but the reviewer argues the actors are wasted in poorly written roles. Jha described the film as a mirthless and worthless excursion into high-strung heroics.
