Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 outperforms Ranabaali at the box office

Jailer 2 faces ₹40 crore OTT cut over North India multiplex release

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 faces a reported ₹40 crore reduction from its ₹160 crore Amazon Prime Video digital rights deal.
