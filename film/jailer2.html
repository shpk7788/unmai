Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 targets massive 125 crore worldwide opening day

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has arrived in cinemas with strong advance bookings, raising expectations of becoming a new Tamil industry hit. Rajinikanth starrer Jailer 2 nears Rs 500 crore in five days

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 is dominating the box office, racing towards the Rs 500-crore mark globally within just five days of release. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the film recorded the highest-ever Monday collection in Tamil cinema history by earning Rs 42.35 crore net on day five due to festive holidays. This massive performance propelled the film's worldwide gross to Rs 477 crore, making it the third-highest-grossing Indian film of 2026 so far. The sequel features returning stars like Ramya Krishnan alongside new additions such as S. J. Suryah and high-profile cameos.

Jailer 2 actor Namo Narayana quit his IT career for cinema

Actor Namo Narayana, known for playing Moorthy in Rajinikanth's Jailer 2, left behind a lucrative software career in the US to pursue filmmaking. He initially worked as an executive producer on the 2009 film Nadodigal before stumbling into acting when director Samuthirakani created a small role for him. Over 15 years, he appeared in more than 80 films before achieving a major breakout in Nelson's Jailer 2.
