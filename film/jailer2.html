Hrithik Roshan makes Tamil debut with a seven-minute cameo in Jailer 2

Bollywood superstar Hrithik Roshan makes his Tamil cinema debut playing a tactical arms operator named Roddy in Nelson Dilipkumar's Jailer 2. His high-impact cameo clocks in at approximately 7 minutes and intersects with the Kalpak syndicate. The film marks an on-screen reunion for Rajinikanth and Hrithik after exactly 40 years, following their 1986 Hindi classic Bhagwaan Dada. Shah Rukh Khan was initially discussed for the sequence before scheduling conflicts led the makers to cast Hrithik Roshan.

Why did Nelson Dilipkumar cut Santhanam from Jailer 2?

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has grossed over Rs. 650 crores worldwide. Reports indicate that director Nelson Dilipkumar removed a 10 to 15 minute comical cameo featuring Santhanam set before Rajinikanth enters Kalpak House in Goa. The cuts were made because the director wanted to focus strictly on action and character elevation after facing criticism during the first film. The Sun Pictures production features music by Anirudh Ravichander and a star-studded cast including Mohanlal and SJ Suryah.

Hrithik Roshan takes zero salary for his Jailer 2 cameo

Bollywood star Hrithik Roshan acted for free in his Jailer 2 cameo after Rajinikanth intervened. Initial industry reports had claimed the actor charged Rs 25 crore for his brief appearance as Roddy, sparking discussions about celebrity cameo economics. Insiders revealed that Sun Pictures was hesitant about the steep market rate, leading to Rajinikanth's intervention. Hrithik ultimately waived his entire fee as a gesture of gratitude and respect for his Bhagwaan Dada co-star.
