Jailer 2 becomes the first Tamil film to cross Rs 800 crore worldwide

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2, directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, has grossed over Rs 800 crore worldwide to become the first Tamil film to reach the milestone. The sequel achieved strong box office numbers across South Indian markets, crossing Rs 200 crore in Tamil Nadu and Rs 75 crore in Kerala. Telugu states and Karnataka are also nearing the Rs 100 crore mark. The film stars Mohanlal, Shivarajkumar, Mithun Chakraborty, SJ Suryah, Vidya Balan, and Ramya Krishnan alongside Rajinikanth.

Why Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 missed its Rs13-billion box-office potential

Rajinikanth's blockbuster sequel Jailer 2 has grossed approximately Rs8.12 billion worldwide after 19 days in theatres, becoming the first Tamil film to cross the Rs8-billion global mark. However, veteran distributor Tiruppur Subramaniam revealed that restrictions on early morning screenings and ticket pricing cost the production up to Rs60 million and prevented the film from reaching its Rs13-billion potential. He noted that previous hits like 2.0 and Leo benefited from flexible pricing and early shows, advantages which Jailer 2 lacked. The film is directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and produced by Sun Pictures.

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 breaks Pushpa 2 record in Karnataka

Superstar Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has become the highest-grossing Kannada-dubbed film ever, surpassing Allu Arjun's Pushpa 2: The Rule. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the film has grossed Rs. 9.5 crores through its Kannada version and aims to cross Rs. 10 crores. However, this milestone may be short-lived as the upcoming movie Ramayana releases this Friday. Backed by Yash's massive stardom, Ramayana has already collected Rs. 3 crores in advance bookings for its Kannada version and is expected to eclipse Jailer 2 within two days.
