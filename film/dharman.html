Rajinikanth's team shuts down rumours of rifts on Dharman sets

Rajinikanth's team has dismissed recent social media rumors claiming a rift between the actor and director Ashwath Marimuthu on the sets of Dharman. The project, produced by Kamal Haasan, previously saw director changes before Ashwath took over.
