Raiza Wilson stars in upcoming fantasy horror thriller Nova

Raiza Wilson and Dhurva Neni play the lead roles in the upcoming Tamil film Nova. Written and directed by Milo, the project belongs to the fantasy horror, science-fiction, and thriller genres. Adhvithi Shetty and Hridika Biswas also star in lead roles, alongside supporting cast members including Sendrayan and Joe John Chacko. Yuvaraj Chandran handles the music composition, while Christo Bob manages cinematography and Prem B looks after editing.
