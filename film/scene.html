Will Suriya's Scene bring back 6 am FDFS shows in Tamil Nadu?

Reports suggest that Suriya's upcoming Deepavali release Scene might bring back 6 am first-day-first-show screenings in Tamil Nadu. Early morning shows were restricted to 9 am following a 2023 government order during the releases of Varisu and Thunivu. While some reports indicate the rule could change for Scene, others claim it may only happen for the Dhanush-starrer Om releasing on November 20. There has been no official announcement from the government yet.

Can Suriya score a hat-trick of 200-crore hits this Diwali?

Actor Suriya is gearing up for his third theatrical release of the year, Scene, directed by Jithu Madhavan. The movie arrives worldwide as a Diwali special on November 6.
