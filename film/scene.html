Nazriya Nazim returns to Tamil cinema after 12 years to play his ally Maya, while Naslen makes his Tamil debut and first villain role as the killer Eswaran. Reviewers are deeply split over the film's pacing, slapstick humour, and whether Naslen's boy-next-door killer has enough substance. While outlets like Pinkvilla praised the experimental tone and Nazriya's powerful return, others found the narrative uneven and its investigation reliant on convenient coincidences.
