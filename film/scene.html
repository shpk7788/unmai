Can Suriya score a hat-trick of 200-crore hits this Diwali?

Actor Suriya is gearing up for his third theatrical release of the year, Scene, directed by Jithu Madhavan. The movie arrives worldwide as a Diwali special on November 6. Earlier this year, his film Karuppu grossed over 300 crores worldwide, while Vishwanath and Sons crossed 200 crores. If Scene also crosses 200 crores, Suriya will achieve the rare record of delivering three 200-crore grossing films in a single year.

Suriya's Scene hits ₹85 crore domestic gross after strong Monday run

Directed by Jithu Madhavan and starring Suriya as a police officer, the action-comedy Scene earned a domestic gross of ₹85.86 crore in four days. On Day 4, the film added ₹19.46 crore in India from 5,639 shows across 1,411 theatres, with Chennai contributing ₹3.39 crore. Its worldwide opening weekend reached ₹116 crore. The film now aims to sustain its box office run to challenge the ₹310 crore lifetime collection of Suriya's previous hit Karuppu.

Mahesh Babu praises Suriya starrer Scene as box office crosses ₹145 crore

Telugu actor Mahesh Babu took to X to praise the Tamil film Scene, starring Suriya, Nazriya Nazim, and Naslen. Directed by Jithu Madhavan, the movie collected ₹145 crore worldwide gross within three days of its November 6 release. Mahesh Babu wrote on X that he loved the film, calling Suriya fabulous as Inbaraj. The movie is produced by Suriya and Jyothika's 2D Entertainment and Zhagaram Studios.

Suriya and Jyotika celebrate as Scene crosses 100 crore collection

Karthik Subbaraj praises Suriya starrer Scene as a whacky thriller

Filmmaker Karthik Subbaraj has praised Suriya's new film Scene, directed by Jithu Madhavan, calling it a mad, fun, intelligent and whacky thriller. The film features Suriya as retired police officer Inbaraj investigating a murder linked to an old case, with Nazriya Nazim playing assistant Maya and Naslen playing the antagonist Eeshwaran. Karthik lauded Suriya's cool performance and the stellar work of the supporting cast, which includes Lal, Raj Priyan, and Gajaraj. The murder mystery marks Jithu Madhavan's directorial debut in Tamil cinema.
