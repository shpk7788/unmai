Karthik Subbaraj praises Suriya starrer Scene as a whacky thriller

Filmmaker Karthik Subbaraj has praised Suriya's new film Scene, directed by Jithu Madhavan, calling it a mad, fun, intelligent and whacky thriller. The film features Suriya as retired police officer Inbaraj investigating a murder linked to an old case, with Nazriya Nazim playing assistant Maya and Naslen playing the antagonist Eeshwaran. Karthik lauded Suriya's cool performance and the stellar work of the supporting cast, which includes Lal, Raj Priyan, and Gajaraj. The murder mystery marks Jithu Madhavan's directorial debut in Tamil cinema.
