Karthik Subbaraj reveals unused martial arts wedding climax for Dorothy

Director Karthik Subbaraj has revealed that his film Dorothy originally featured a very different climax involving a Madurai wedding and a martial arts fight. Instead of the current ending, the narrative was supposed to show Whistle Veeraya's gang attacking the couple before Dorothy defends Karthi in a Bruce Lee-style action sequence. The film stars Rishikanth and Sananth, and follows childhood friends dealing with caste divides and a trans identity transition.

Netflix film Dorothy tells a bold Tamil transgender gangster story

Netflix film "Dorothy" is a Tamil gangster saga directed by Karthik Subbaraj that features a transgender love story. The story follows childhood friends Karthi and Subbu, who grow up in a harsh village under a local strongman where caste and rigid ideas of manhood dominate. Subbu eventually transitions into a trans woman named Dorothy, becoming the romantic lead of her own story. The film stars Sananth and Rishikanth, with Keerthy Suresh appearing as Dorothy from Kerala. It features cinematography by S. Thirunavukkarasu, editing by Ganesh Siva, and a score by Ilaiyaraaja.

Karthik Subbaraj reveals his original Madurai gangster story for Game Changer

Director Karthik Subbaraj shared details of his original story for Shankar's 2025 film Game Changer, marking the first time he wrote a story for another director. Speaking to Student Tribe, Subbaraj explained that his script centered on an IAS officer trying to change the system with grounded Madurai gangster elements and action, comparing its tone to Newton. He noted that the story dynamics changed when adapted into a big-budget film for star Ram Charan by Shankar. The film also stars Kiara Advani, Anjali, and SJ Suryah.
