Trans activists praise Karthik Subbaraj film Dorothy for honest representation

Transgender activists Sasha and Dr N. Jency praised Karthik Subbaraj's film Dorothy for its emotional depth and accurate portrayal of the community. In an interview, the panellists contrasted Dorothy with older Tamil films that depict trans characters as victims or comic relief, noting that this film offers a rare happy ending and love story. They highlighted that society is quick to pity trans people in distress but struggles to accept them when they study, work, and seek equal rights. The activists also praised the mainstream release for fostering important public conversations about love, gender equality, and patriarchy.
