How Karthik Subbaraj uses animals and landscapes to expose male ego in Dorothy

Karthik Subbaraj's Tamil period drama Dorothy was released on September 25, 2026, starring Sananth, Rishikanth, Keerthy Suresh, and Muthukumar. Set in 1990s rural Madurai, the story follows two friends whose bond is tested by caste and masculinity when Dorothy arrives in their village. The film features several computer-generated animals, including roosters, a scorpion, and a dung beetle, which mirror human violence and social hierarchies. Cinematographer Tirru explained in an interview that human characters were deliberately placed against expansive landscapes to expose their small egos and irrelevant prejudices.
