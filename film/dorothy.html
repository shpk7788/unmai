From a Chennai mansion to stardom: Rishikanth on his 15-year journey

After spending 15 years chasing film dreams in Chennai, actor Rishikanth has become a sensation following his roles in 'Modha Rathri' and the queer narrative 'Dorothy', directed by Karthik Subburaj. Karthik Subbaraj reveals the scrapped original climax for Dorothy

Director Karthik Subbaraj disclosed that his film Dorothy originally featured a different ending involving a wedding in Madurai. In the scrapped sequence, villains interrupted the ceremony over caste differences, leading to a martial arts fight scene by Dorothy. The filmmaker ultimately replaced this elaborate climax with a scene where Karthi accepts Dorothy's identity and shares a kiss with her. The movie stars Rishikanth and Sananth and recently premiered on OTT.
