Karthik Subbaraj's Tamil film Dorothy examines caste and gender violence.

Directed by Karthik Subbaraj, the Tamil film Dorothy examines how caste, class, gender, and family hierarchies shape social relationships and identities. The film follows Karthi, who challenges caste discrimination, yet struggles to accept his friend Subbu when she embraces her identity as a transgender woman. Through such everyday relationships, the movie highlights how individuals can oppose certain prejudices while still reproducing the rigid expectations of a patriarchal society. A character in the film notes, "Who are we to decide who should live? Are we God?" The narrative demonstrates how intimacy and love can expose the unspoken limits of social progressiveness.

Filmmaker Soundarya Rajinikanth took to X to praise the Tamil film Dorothy, directed by Karthik Subbaraj, for its powerful message of equality and acceptance.
