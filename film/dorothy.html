Karthik Subbaraj's drama Dorothy locks in its Netflix release date

Karthik Subbaraj's drama Dorothy will stream on Netflix starting October 23, exactly four weeks after its theatrical release. Although Netflix has not posted an official social media announcement, the film now appears in its library. The story follows two friends named Karthi and Subbu after a woman transforms their perspectives on life. Sananth, Rishikanth, and Keerthy Suresh lead the cast, while Ilaiyaraaja composed the music. Karthik Subbaraj produced the film alongside Guneet Monga, Aachin Jain, and Jyoti Deshpande.
