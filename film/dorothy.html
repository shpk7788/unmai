Sananth deserves awards for his transwoman role in Karthik Subbaraj's Dorothy

Actor Sananth delivers a restrained and precise performance as Subbu, a transwoman, in director Karthik Subbaraj's film Dorothy. While his co-star Rishikanth gained major attention in theatres, Sananth's nuanced portrayal of suppressed identity and femininity stands out now that the movie is streaming on OTT. He skillfully balances Subbu's survival-driven masculine facade with her true feminine identity through subtle physical shifts and body language. Keerthy Suresh plays Dorothy, a sister-in-law whose support and tragic end catalyze Subbu's eventual coming out to her family.

Karthik Subbaraj teams up with Ilaiyaraaja after using his classics for years

Director Karthik Subbaraj is finally collaborating with composer Ilaiyaraaja for his upcoming project Dorothy. Throughout his career, Karthik has frequently incorporated the maestro's timeless tracks into his movies. His debut horror thriller Pizza featured the song Poongaatru Thirumbuma. Later, he used tracks like Thendral Vanthu in Iraivi, Raman Aandalum in Petta, and Maraththa Vechchavan in Mahaan to elevate key moments on screen.

Keerthy Suresh reflects on defying box-office skepticism for Karthik Subbaraj's Dorothy

Actor Keerthy Suresh took on an extended cameo in Karthik Subbaraj's rural Tamil Nadu drama Dorothy despite industry peers warning her it would fail commercially. The 1990s-set film sparked conversations on queer representation and successfully completed a 25-day theatrical run before its Netflix release. Keerthy admitted she faced skepticism over her limited six to seven days of shooting, but prioritized being part of change-making cinema. She noted, I was told the film will not be a box-office hit, but we're talking about 25 days at the box office and now it's on Netflix. Keerthy is also starring in the upcoming Netflix series Akka, directed by Dharmaraj Shetty, which premieres on October 30.

Karthik Subbaraj's Tamil drama Dorothy gives a trans woman a happy ending

Karthik Subbaraj's Tamil drama Dorothy stars Rishikanth and Sananth, featuring Keerthy Suresh as a new arrival midway through the film. The story follows Subbu, who hides her gender identity from her family and Sevapatti village before transitioning into Dorothy. Subbu finds a supportive community among trans women, ultimately embracing her womanhood and a rare happy ending in cinema. Unlike tragic depictions in films like Haddi, Super Deluxe, and Nagarkirtan, Subbaraj's film offers hope and love for its trans protagonist.

Keerthy Suresh starrer Dorothy begins streaming on Netflix

Keerthy Suresh starrer Dorothy is now streaming on Netflix in five languages starting October 23, 2026. The Tamil crime drama, directed by Karthik Subbaraj, is set in rural Madurai during the early 1990s and follows two childhood friends whose bond is tested by the arrival of a woman named Dorothy. The film was made on a budget of 7 crore rupees and grossed 18 crore rupees in worldwide collections after releasing in theatres on September 25.
