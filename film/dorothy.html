Karthik Subbaraj reveals the scrapped original climax of Dorothy

Director Karthik Subbaraj revealed that his film Dorothy originally featured a different climax set in Madurai with a wedding between Karthi and Dorothy. In the scrapped ending, villain Whistle Veeraya's gang attacks the couple over caste differences, leading to a Bruce Lee-style martial arts fight by Dorothy. Karthik eventually dropped this elaborate sequence and ended the film with Karthi accepting Dorothy's identity and sharing a kiss. The movie stars Rishikanth and Sananth and recently premiered on OTT.

Sananth and Rishikanth star in Netflix crime drama Dorothy

The South Indian film Dorothy revolves around two friends, Karthi and Subbu, growing up in a violent, patriarchal village. While Karthi leans into criminality, Subbu moves towards peace and emotional awareness. The movie features performances by Sananth and Rishikanth, alongside a brief appearance by Keerthy Suresh. Ilaiyaraaja provides the music, and the project carries the cinematic style of Karthik Subbaraj. The film is currently streaming on Netflix.

Sananth opens up about the challenges of playing a transgender role in Dorothy

Actor Sananth discussed the heavy responsibility of portraying a transgender character named Subbu in Karthick Subbaraj's Dorothy. He credited the director's trust for giving him the confidence to take on the demanding role after auditioning alongside co-star Rishikanth. Sananth prepared by interacting with transgender individuals and observing women to ensure an authentic portrayal. "Honestly, this character is a great blessing for me," the actor said. Viewers moved by the performance offered emotional responses during screenings, which deeply touched him.
