Radhika Sarathkumar starrer Delivery Boy locks October 23 release date

Director Nani's emotional action entertainer Delivery Boy will release worldwide in theatres on October 23. The film stars Leo Sivakumar, Brigida, and Radhika Sarathkumar in the lead roles, alongside Dushyanth, Kaali Venkat, and Bose Venkat. Director Nani stated that the plot follows a lower middle class delivery boy trying to save his mother. The 42-day shoot was completed across Chennai and Coimbatore before wrapping up in December last year.
