Director Balaji Tharaneetharan opens up about working with Vijay Sethupathi in Baththa

Director Balaji Tharaneetharan has teamed up with Vijay Sethupathi for the third time in the Tamil film Baththa, which is co-produced by Atlee and his wife. The filmmaker developed the story drawing from childhood memories, such as accidentally locking his teacher in a school washroom during the second grade. He noted that Sethupathi remains unchanged as a friendly and collaborative actor over their 15-year association. Tharaneetharan described the star by stating, 'He's been the same friendly person I got to know years back.'

Vijay Sethupathi stars as a village rowdy trapped in a school toilet in Baththa

Vijay Sethupathi plays a feared village rowdy named Baththa in the 1986-set feel-good entertainer directed by Balaji Tharaneetharan. After a schoolboy named Sabari accidentally locks Baththa inside a school toilet during the holidays, the trapped gangster survives on powdered rusk slipped through the door. This unusual confinement forces the violent thug to reflect on his actions, appreciate his wife Kanimma, and rethink his life. The film also stars Sahasra Shree, Lijomol Jose, and Sai Prithviraj, with music composed by Sai Abhyankkar.

Vijay Sethupathi stars in Balaji Tharaneetharan's feel-good drama Baththa

Vijay Sethupathi stars as a feared village rowdy named Baththa in the feel-good drama Baththa, directed by Balaji Tharaneetharan and set in 1986. A schoolboy named Sabari accidentally locks Baththa inside a dingy school toilet during the holidays, keeping him alive by slipping in powdered rusk. Being trapped forces the violent rowdy to reflect on his deeds, miss his wife Kanimma and daughter Thenu, and undergo an unexpected redemption. The film also features Sahasra Shree, Lijomol Jose, Santhy Balachandran, Hari Krishnan, and Sai Prithviraj, with music composed by Sai Abhyankkar.

Vijay Sethupathi starrer Baththa struggles at the box office despite positive reviews

Vijay Sethupathi's Baththa, directed by Balaji Tharaneetharan and produced by Atlee, was released on October 1st, 2026. Despite receiving strong reviews from critics and audiences, the film has collected only around 12 crore rupees in India. Trade experts attribute the modest collection to poor promotion and the film's unique emotional gangster concept failing to resonate in B and C centres. With Jailer 2 releasing on October 15th, the film has a limited window to pick up its box office numbers.
