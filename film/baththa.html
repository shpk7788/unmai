Vijay Sethupathi starrer Baththa struggles at the box office despite positive reviews

Vijay Sethupathi's Baththa, directed by Balaji Tharaneetharan and produced by Atlee, was released on October 1st, 2026. Despite receiving strong reviews from critics and audiences, the film has collected only around 12 crore rupees in India. Trade experts attribute the modest collection to poor promotion and the film's unique emotional gangster concept failing to resonate in B and C centres. With Jailer 2 releasing on October 15th, the film has a limited window to pick up its box office numbers.
