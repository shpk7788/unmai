Soori starrer Mandaadi makes its OTT debut on Netflix

Tamil actor Soori's sports action drama Mandaadi has arrived on Netflix following its theatrical release on September 10, 2026. The film grossed over 150 crore rupees globally during its box office run, marking a major milestone for the lead actor. Directed by Mathimaran Pugazhendhi, the multilingual project features music by GV Prakash Kumar and co-stars Suhas, Mahima Nambiar, and Sathyaraj. It is currently streaming in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, and Malayalam.

Soori starrer Mandaadi gets IMAX re-release despite Netflix premiere

Director Mathimaran Pugazhendhi's Mandaadi is returning to IMAX screens on October 9 following audience requests, just days after Netflix announced its October 8 streaming premiere. The coastal drama starring Soori and Suhas has crossed 150 crore at the worldwide box office, making it Soori's first film to cross this milestone as a lead actor. Actor Soori announced the re-release on social media, writing that the banger experience is returning to IMAX. The film is produced by Elred Kumar and features music by GV Prakash.
