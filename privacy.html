Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Unmai does not ask you to create an account. Your likes, saved posts, poll votes and settings are stored only in your own browser and are never sent to us.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. Our host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, site icons from Google's favicon service and film images from TMDB. These services may see your IP address when your browser requests those files. If we add advertising or analytics in future, this page will be updated first, including how advertisers such as Google may use cookies and how you can opt out.
